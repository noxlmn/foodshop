Our Story

From a Food Truck to Your Front Door

A Unique Food Supply began in 2011 when Chef Marcus Rivera started serving gourmet tacos from a converted food truck in downtown San Francisco. What set him apart wasn't just the food — it was the experience. He cooked everything fresh, right in front of his customers.

Word spread. Soon, clients were asking him to bring that same live-cooking magic to their weddings, corporate events, and private parties. By 2015, we had grown into a full-service catering company with a team of 15 professional chefs, each specializing in different cuisines.

Today, we serve over 150 events per year across California, Nevada, and Arizona. Whether you want us to cook live at your venue or deliver a perfectly prepared feast, we bring the same passion, precision, and freshness to every plate.
